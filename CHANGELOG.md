# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-18

- **First public release.** The repository's history starts with this version; 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 were private and are described below.
- **The old training panel is gone** (stage E7, after the first real run on Ubuntu): `wakeword train-ui`, `make ui` and `make ui-remote`, its page (`wakeword/trainer/static`), its access token (`data/panel.json`) and the whole SSH mode (`SshRunner`, rsync of the code and the household to the machine, "Send to machine", "Fetch models", "Fetch samples", `data/trainer.json`). The studio trains on the GPU machine itself; `wakeword/trainer` is now only its backend (`Trainer`, `LocalRunner`, progress, models).
- The language packs describe the studio: the cards' help (machine, words, data, training, models, results, live, log) and every text that named the old panel's cards or buttons were rewritten; 46 texts only the old panel used were removed, and so was the old panel's long "guide" help. A test keeps the old panel out of every text.
- Docs: `guide.md` (the old panel's guide) and its 34 screenshots removed; the FAQ sections of the panel became the studio's ("errors and messages", "how to do it in the studio"); chapters 01, 06, the cheat sheet and the README point to the studio. `docs/screenshots.py` became `docs/demo_household.py` (the made-up household of the demo, the tests and the screenshots); `make screenshots` makes the studio's pictures only.
- `household.yaml` written by the studio starts with "# Wake word household - edited in the wake word studio".
- **Docs: 13. The studio step by step** (PL + EN): a map of every function, the first word from scratch in 12 steps, adding a word, training another word and again, evaluating, export to Home Assistant, the word on Voice PE, a checklist; 9 new screenshots of the studio (`make screenshots`); the demo household has two kept runs for Models. A test keeps every node of the tree in the guide.
- The studio no longer points to the old panel's cards ("card 3", "card 6") or its "Fetch models" / "Send to machine" buttons: 22 shared texts have the studio's own wording, and the studio's words win in the views. The theme label in ⚙ says "Theme".
- ⚙ in the top bar opens a pop-up under itself (theme as a segmented switch, language, "All look and language") instead of a dialog; the choice works at once and the view stays. The full settings are a new tree node, Settings → Look and language. ⚙ and ☰ close with Esc or a click outside, and each closes the other (the shared standard, §5).
- Tests no longer read the real ESPHome folder of the Home Assistant share (`make test` exports `WAKEWORD_ESPHOME`).
- **Fixed: TensorFlow in the image did not see the GPU** (found on the first real run on Ubuntu, 2026-11-18): TensorFlow 2.21's `libtensorflow_framework.so.2` has no `nvidia/cusolver/lib` in its RUNPATH, so it missed `libcusolver.so.11` and would have trained on the CPU without a word. The image sets `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` to that folder and the build checks the file. torch and torchaudio now come from the PyTorch CUDA 12 index (`TORCH_INDEX`, one CUDA family in the image instead of CUDA 12 + 13), and the build fails if any non-CUDA-12 `nvidia-*` package gets in.
- `docker/compose.yaml` builds the named stage `studio` (`target`), never the last stage by chance; `/api/health` says which app answers (`app`, `mode` studio/demo, `version`).
- Installer: checks that the driver answers and is at least 525, names the user with `$(id -un)` (`$USER` may be unset), checks TensorFlow on the GPU at the end (`--gpu-check`, also in `make status`); `--stop` (`make stop`) and `--uninstall [--yes]` (`make uninstall`, `CONFIRM=yes`: lists what goes, asks for yes, keeps the data, keeps the code folder when data lies inside, never touches another project).
- Tests: the installer on a pretend machine (no driver, silent or old driver, no Docker, no compose, no access, no toolkit, a failed build, a full install and a second run, TensorFlow without the GPU, stop, uninstall), the runner starting the real `train/train.sh`, the demo's health.
- README: the introduction and the first screenshot show the studio, not the old panel (`make ui`, gone in 1.0.0); a test keeps it so.

Every card of the panel is in it (`docs/design/parity.md`, checked by a test). `wakeword studio`, `make studio`, `make demo`.

- `train/train.sh` and the household test take `VENV` (the image brings the training environment ready).
- **The shared standard of the GPU machine's apps** (`~/Home Assistant/standards/gpu-studio.md`, agreed with the Assist Router trainer and Piper Voice Cloner): the same make targets (`install`, `deploy`, `login`, `logs`, `status`, `docker-up|down|logs|shell`, `demo` on port 8766, `test-browser`, `screenshots`, `docs`, `docs-serve`, `icons`, `lint`), the same login, top bar, power-off and documentation.

The `?` in the top bar opens it; the `?` of every card and field links to its chapter's anchor. Replaces the hand-written `docs/<lang>/index.html` guides and `make docs-pages` (copies in `backup/`).
- Help: only the `?` in the top bar opens the documentation; the one `?` of every card opens a small window with its text (✕, Esc, a click beside it), with “More in the guide” to its chapter's anchor. The `?` next to the training fields and the old “How to train?” window are gone (fields have hints).
- The left and the right column fold (‹ / ›, Ctrl/⌘+B and Ctrl/⌘+Alt+B) into a narrow strip with an unfold button; remembered by the browser, the right one starts folded in a narrow window (gpu-studio standard).
- **The same commands as every app** (gpu-studio standard, section 2): `make help` starts with “1. Start” (`demo`, `demo-clean`, `docs`, `docs-serve`, `doctor` - only Docker Desktop), then “2. GPU machine” and “3. Development”. `make docs`, `docs-build` and `docs-serve` (http://127.0.0.1:9766) run in Docker, from the `docs` stage of the Dockerfile - no Python needed. `make setup` makes `.venv` through uv (it fetches Python 3.12 itself; no brew). `make test` no longer builds images, `make test-docker` does (pytest marker `docker`); `make test-all` = lint + test + test-browser + test-docker; `make format`; `make lint` also checks the formatting; `make doctor` checks Docker, git, ssh, rsync, the GPU machine, uv, Chrome and installs nothing.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-09

First version.

- Household file: people (adult/child), wake words shared by several people, up to two words per device.
- Web recorder (HTTPS, phones) with delivery-variant instructions in English and Polish.
- Import of any audio format, quality check as a person × word matrix, held-out test split.
- Voice expansion (pitch/formant and tempo) and augmentation preview, numpy only.
- Synthetic speech script (piper-sample-generator + native Piper voices reading phonetic spellings).
- microWakeWord training config with the household's other words as negatives; training recipe for a GPU machine.
- Evaluation: FRR per person and group, false wakes per hour, cutoff table and recommendation.
- Export: model manifest v2 and an ESPHome snippet per device.
- Home Assistant integration *Wake Word Recorder*: record takes through voice satellites (Voice PE) with spoken instructions, own recording pipeline, automatic restore of the satellite's pipeline.
- Wake Word Recorder: background recording through a satellite (`start_background`, default 30 min) to `data/background/`; household.yaml created from HA people when missing; household.yaml changes are picked up automatically.
- `wakeword train-ui`: "Home Assistant Wakeword" training panel in the browser — this computer or a GPU machine over SSH (key picker, rsync, detached training), live stage/GPU/metrics with verdicts, final test with the recommended cutoff, fetch, evaluate, export.
- Training recipe: `tensorflow[and-cuda]` (GPU), stops when TensorFlow sees no GPU (`ALLOW_CPU=1`), `TRAIN=0` converts the best weights only.
- Training panel: "Family and words" card — people (adult/child, takes per word), words (name, language, who uses it, spellings, sound-alikes), devices (two slots, picked from the voice devices found in the esphome folder next to the household) and targets, validated and saved to household.yaml (backup kept); works with a household without words or without a file yet.
- Training panel: cards fold (click the heading, remembered per browser; a configured machine starts folded and shows user@host); a word id can be changed with its recordings (folder and recordings.csv), prepared data goes to data/backup; settings explain the targets and the held-out takes.
- Training panel: "Recordings" tab — every take and home background on disk grouped by word and person (also words/people no longer in the household), listen, select, delete; deleted files go to data/backup/deleted-<time>/.
- Training panel: people and words are reordered by drag and drop (handle ⠿, or ← → keys on it); the order is written to household.yaml and is the order everywhere else.
- Wake Word Recorder: "speak now" signal after each instruction (select: beep, HA announcement chime, none) — its own text-to-speech in the recording pipeline speaks with the usual voice and appends the signal only when the satellite listens next.
- Word detection (integration and toolkit, identical and cross-tested): the background level is measured in the recording; the word is the strongest segment standing out from it (pauses up to 0.5 s inside, edges followed down to 4 dB above the noise), so background noise and separate sounds no longer make a take "too long"; the noise under the word stays in the take. New issue `too_noisy` (word less than 8 dB above the background).

- Training panel: the words ticked in “Words to train” are kept over a reload (browser storage); only the first visit ticks the prepared ones.
- Prepare keeps what the word was prepared with (`data/build/<word>/prepared.json`); the panel refuses a training after spellings, languages, sound-alikes or voices changed until the word is prepared again, and Prepare moves ElevenLabs takes no longer chosen to `data/backup` (before only a new ElevenLabs run did). Removing a language asks first and says so.
- Changing a word's id keeps its ElevenLabs takes, the build with trained and kept models and the synthetic speech under the new id — here and on the GPU machine (over SSH); Prepare's files go to `data/backup`.
- Devices tab: fields aligned at the top, the buttons level with them, the name on its own line on a phone; every select drawn the same height as the text fields (Safari); hints say the devices come from Home Assistant.
- The GPU machine's power through Home Assistant: a machine switch (example package `docs/home-assistant/maszyna_gpu.yaml`: on = its plug, off = SSH poweroff, wait below 5 W, then the plug) and the plug's power sensor in Settings → Home Assistant; a header button with the state (off / starting / on · W / shutting down) switches it, never during a training.
- Card 5: weights of the sets (sampling_weight per set, `SET_WEIGHTS` of train.sh) with the presets Family (household voices and home sounds, negative weight 30) and Neutral (synthetic and ElevenLabs voices); kept with the model (`run.json` "weights") and shown in the models table; the training plan line carries them.
- Household test: about every 4th home background recording (by file name) is kept out of training; `wakeword evaluate-models <word> --missing` measures every kept model on the family's held-out takes per person and that test background (`household.json` next to the model), run by train.sh after every training and by "Test at home" in the Models card. The Models card shows a Household and a Neutral column (each "× from the goal", cutoff and results) and ranks by the one chosen.
- Models card: the list is replaced only when the new one has arrived (no flicker); folding a word is saved from the click only.
- Training panel: the page loads at once also when the GPU machine is off — the machine (SSH, outside the lock) and Home Assistant are checked in a background thread, the header says “Checking the machine…” meanwhile; Home Assistant's name is looked up once (IPv4 first, kept 5 min) instead of a 5 s mDNS wait on every request.
- A token Home Assistant rejects (401) is asked once only: every 401 is a failed login for HA's ip_ban, so the panel stops all questions to HA (machine state, entities, devices, household.yaml, recordings - also in jobs) and says “Home Assistant rejected the token”; “Save” in Settings → Home Assistant clears it and checks the connection with one request.
- Power settings: the machine switch and the power sensor are lists of Home Assistant's entities, “name (entity id)” sorted by name (“Loading entities from HA…” meanwhile, “↻ Suggestions from HA” asks again, a saved id HA no longer lists stays, typed by hand when HA gives no list) - the same as in piper-voice-cloner.
- Wake Word Recorder: every wake of a satellite is logged to `/config/assist/wake_word_log.csv` — word, room, pipeline, what was heard and the outcome (`no_speech`, `command`, `duplicate`, …) — to measure how often each word wakes each room on its own.
